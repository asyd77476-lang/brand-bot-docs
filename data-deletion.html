You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you (messages, comments, order and delivery details).

How to request deletion

  1. Email mohameddahmedd602@gmail.com (or message our page) with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the mobile number you used for your order and/or your Facebook / Instagram profile name, so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm the request and delete your data from our systems, including our order spreadsheet.

We aim to complete requests within 30 days. Some order records may need to be kept longer where accounting or legal rules require it; in that case we will tell you.
