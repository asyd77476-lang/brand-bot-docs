Privacy Policy

Style Wear Demo · Last updated: 2026-11-09

1. Who we are

Style Wear Demo ("we", "us") runs customer-service channels on Facebook, Instagram and our website. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you message us, comment on our posts, or place an order. Contact: mohameddahmedd602@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Messages and public comments you send to our pages, and your public profile name provided by the platform.
  • Order details you give us: name, mobile number, delivery address, products, size and quantity.
  • Images or voice messages you choose to send us.
  • Technical logs (for example error logs) needed to keep the service running.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and process and deliver your orders.
  • To pass complaints or complex requests to a human team member.
  • To monitor and improve the quality and safety of our replies.

4. Automated assistant (AI)

Many replies are written by an automated AI assistant. Your message may be processed by Google's Gemini API to produce a reply. Please do not send payment card numbers, ID documents or other sensitive information in chat.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the service: Meta (Facebook / Instagram), Google (Gemini API; Google Sheets for order records), our hosting provider, and delivery partners when needed to deliver your order.

6. Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil the order and meet accounting or legal obligations. Other conversation data is kept only as long as needed for support and quality purposes, and is deleted on request.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time: see data deletion or contact mohameddahmedd602@gmail.com.

8. Security

Access to customer data is restricted to authorised staff and protected by passwords. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what is needed.

9. Children

Our service is not directed at children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
